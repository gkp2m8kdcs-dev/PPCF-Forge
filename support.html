Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common support questions.

How do I back up my data?

PPCF Forge stores data locally. You have three options:

  1. iCloud Backup — enable iCloud Backup for your iPhone in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Backup. The App's data is included in your encrypted device backup.
  2. CSV export — go to Settings → Export Data to generate CSV files that you can save anywhere (Files, iCloud Drive, email).
  3. iCloud Drive — choose iCloud Drive as the export location to sync the CSV file across your devices.
How do I delete all my data?

Open the App and navigate to Settings → Data → Erase All Data. This permanently removes every transaction, receipt, portfolio entry, budget, and goal from your device. The App will return to a fresh-install state.

This action cannot be undone. Export first if you want a backup.

I lost my data after reinstalling the App. Can you restore it?

Because we do not operate any servers that store user data, we cannot restore data from our side. If you had an iCloud or device backup taken before the reinstall, restoring that backup from your iPhone will bring the data back. CSV exports are also a reliable way to re-import your history.

How do I attach a photo receipt to a transaction?

Open any transaction and choose Add Receipt. You can either take a new photo with the camera (the App will request permission) or pick one from your photo library. Photos are stored locally on your device.

Does the App sync across my devices?

PPCF Forge is a single-device, on-device app by design — it does not sync between devices because that would require a remote service that holds your financial data. If you install the App on another iPhone, you can manually export from one and import the CSV on the other.

How do I change the currency?

Go to Settings → Currency & Region and pick your preferred currency symbol and decimal format. The change applies across all screens immediately.

The charts aren't rendering smoothly. What should I do?

Try the following in order:

  1. Restart the App (swipe up from the app switcher).
  2. Restart your iPhone.
  3. Make sure your iOS version is 16 or later (Settings → General → Software Update).
  4. If the issue persists, send us your device model, iOS version, and a screen recording to a45984849@gmail.com.
How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find PPCF Forge in your purchase history, and choose Report a Problem. Apple handles all refund decisions.

How do I delete my account?

PPCF Forge has no account system — there's nothing to delete on our side. To erase all App data, use Settings → Data → Erase All Data, or simply delete the App from your iPhone.
