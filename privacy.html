Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

PPCF Forge ("we", "our", "the App") is a fully on-device iPhone finance and wealth tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what data the App handles, how it is handled, and — importantly — what data we do not collect. By using PPCF Forge, you agree to the practices described below.

Short version

PPCF Forge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your financial data. All your transactions, receipts, and portfolio entries are stored locally on your iPhone. We have no servers that receive your data.

1. Overview

PPCF Forge is designed around the principle that your financial information is personal and should remain on your device. The App's architecture is intentionally offline-first: there is no account system, no remote database, no analytics pipeline, and no advertising network.

Because we do not collect your data in the first place, we cannot sell, share, or lose it. This page documents that design in detail.

2. What Data We Collect

In short: nothing leaves your device by default. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, government IDs)
  • Account or sign-in information, because there is no account
  • Financial data you enter into the App (transactions, balances, receipts, portfolio holdings)
  • Device identifiers used to track you (advertising ID, IDFA, MAID)
  • Location, contacts, or browsing history
  • In-app behavior, clickstream, or session telemetry
  • Crash reports tied to your identity

The only data we may receive from you is information you actively choose to send us — for example, the contents of a support email sent to a45984849@gmail.com.

3. What Data We Do Not Collect

To be explicit, the App does not include or rely on:

  • Third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Crash reporting SDKs that send user identifiers (Apple's own optional, anonymous crash reports are governed by your system settings)
  • Advertising networks or remarketing pixels
  • Social login SDKs
  • Remote backend services that store user content

4. App Permissions

The App requests the following iOS permissions only when needed and only at the moment you trigger the related action. None of these permissions are used to collect data about you remotely.

PermissionPurposeStored Locally?
Camera Capture a photo of a receipt to attach to a transaction Yes — saved to your Photos library or app's local storage
Photo Library Attach an existing photo as a receipt Yes — only the photo you select
Notifications (optional) Send optional budget or goal reminders you configure Reminders are local; no remote push servers are operated by us

You may revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security on your iPhone.

5. Local Storage & Backups

All financial data you enter is stored in an on-device SQLite database inside the App's sandboxed container. Photos you attach are stored either in your Photos library (if you choose "Save to Photos") or in the App's local sandbox.

If you enable iCloud Backup on your iPhone, iOS may include the App's data in your encrypted device backup. Those backups are governed by Apple's iCloud terms and your Apple ID settings, not by PPCF Forge. We do not have access to your iCloud backups.

You can export all of your data as CSV files at any time from the App's Settings screen. You can also delete all data with a single tap.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services. The only external services the App interacts with are:

  • Apple App Store — for distribution, governed by Apple's policies.
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs) — only if you opt in to local reminder notifications; no remote account is required.

We do not share your data with any third party because we do not have your data to share.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, this requirement is satisfied by design.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. If a change is material, we will surface a notice inside the App on the next launch. Your continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
