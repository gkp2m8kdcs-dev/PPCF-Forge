Privacy isn't a feature. It's the whole product.

PPCF Forge is built around one principle: your financial data belongs to you, and only to you.

✓

Fully on-device storage

Every transaction, receipt, and portfolio entry is stored in a local SQLite database on your iPhone. We don't have servers that store your data.

✓

No account, no sign-up

Open the app and you're in. We don't collect your email, name, phone, or any identifier to use PPCF Forge.

✓

No analytics or tracking SDKs

The app does not contain third-party analytics, ad networks, or tracking SDKs. There is no behavioral telemetry leaving your device.

✓

You control your data

Export everything as CSV any time. Clear all data with a single tap. Backups are stored only where you choose — iCloud Drive, Files, or email.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about PPCF Forge.

Does PPCF Forge require an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account of any kind. You install the app and start tracking immediately.

Where is my financial data stored?

All data is stored locally on your iPhone in an on-device database. Photos you attach to receipts are saved to your photo library or the app's secure local storage. We do not operate servers that receive your financial data.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You can export all transactions, budgets, and portfolio entries as CSV files at any time from the Settings screen.

Does PPCF Forge connect to my bank?

No. PPCF Forge is a manual tracker by design. There are no bank-linking features, no credentials to enter, and no third-party data feeds.

How do I contact support?

Email us at a45984849@gmail.com or visit the Support page. We reply within two business days.

What iOS version is required?

PPCF Forge requires iOS 16 or later on iPhone.
